Drop your product CSV here or click to choose · Shopify or WooCommerce product export · nothing is sent anywhere

What it checks

  • Below cost — sells for less than it costs you.
  • No cost recorded — the platform drops these from profit reports, so your profit looks better than it is.
  • Under your target margin.
  • Price inversions — a "compare at" price that is not actually higher.
  • Published but out of stock — customers can order what you cannot ship.
  • Duplicate SKUs, especially at different prices.
  • Supplier price changes — which products' margins moved, and which now lose money.

What it will not do

  • It will not guess a missing cost. A product with no cost is reported as unknown and left out of the totals — never counted as free.
  • It will not change anything in your store. It only reads a file you chose.
  • There is no AI in the arithmetic. Every figure is plain deterministic calculation you could redo by hand.
Planning a sale? Discount Margin Guard takes the same export and shows which products would sell below cost at the discounts you intend to run — before the sale starts. A 25% code on a 20% automatic discount is 40% off, not 45%.
Reconciling payouts? PayoutProof turns the Shopify Payments transactions export into a per-payout breakdown of charges, refunds, adjustments and fees, checked against the payout total Shopify reported.

MarginProof is independent and not affiliated with Shopify, WooCommerce or any platform. It reads a file you select and never transmits it.
