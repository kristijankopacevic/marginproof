Will your sale survive your costs?

Enter the discounts you plan to run. See exactly which products would sell below cost — before the sale starts, not after.

Discounts combine on the reduced price, not by adding up. A 25% code on top of a 20% automatic discount is 40% off, not 45%. On a £40 product costing £23 that leaves £1 a unit — before shipping, fees and returns. That is the arithmetic this page does for your whole catalogue.
Drop your product CSV here or click to choose · Shopify or WooCommerce export · nothing leaves your browser

How it works

  • Break-even discount — the discount at which a product earns exactly nothing. It is the same number as its margin.
  • Safe ceiling — the deepest site-wide discount that keeps every priced product above cost. It is set by your thinnest product, not the average.
  • Stacking — each discount applies to the already-reduced price, so they compound rather than add.

What it will not do

  • It will not guess a missing cost. Products with no cost are reported as cannot tell and left out of the totals — never assumed safe.
  • It will not tell you your total loss. A product export contains no sales volumes, so every figure here is per unit.
  • It does not read or change your store. It only reads a file you chose.
